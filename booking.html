Frequently Asked Questions

School Van Booking FAQs

How do I request a school van?

Enter the parent contact information, school details, pickup locality, destination and preferred schedule in the form.

Can I request one-way school transport?

Yes. The form includes options for one-way and round-trip school transport.

Is the booking confirmed after submitting?

No. This page is a frontend design only. A working booking system needs a backend and provider confirmation.

Does this page provide live van tracking?

No. Live tracking requires a connected GPS source, backend service, suitable permissions and a map interface.
