Before using live tracking

  • Confirm that your school or transport provider offers GPS tracking.
  • Use the official parent account rather than sharing private trip links publicly.
  • Check when the location was last updated before relying on it.
  • Contact the school if a van is delayed or tracking information is unavailable.

Help centre

Live Van Tracking FAQs

Answers to common questions about school van location and trip updates.

How can I track my child's school van?

Sign in to the authorised parent dashboard and select the assigned trip if your school provides GPS tracking. This sample page does not display a real vehicle location.

Does SchoolVan show the van's live location?

The current page is a front-end demo. Live location requires a GPS device or authorised mobile location source, a secure backend, and a connected map or tracking service.

Can I see the estimated arrival time?

An estimated arrival time can only be provided when the system has suitable current location, route and travel-time data. This demo does not calculate arrival times.

What should I do if van tracking is not updating?

Check the last update time and contact your school or authorised transport operator. Do not rely on a stale location as proof of a vehicle's current position.

Is the school van's location public?

Real student transport location data should be restricted to authorised users. Avoid publishing live vehicle locations, student details or private trip links on public pages.
