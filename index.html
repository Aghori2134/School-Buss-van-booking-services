Frequently Asked Questions

Things you might want to know.

Who can use SchoolVan?

The concept is designed for parents, students, schools and school van drivers serving Aurangabad.

Can I register if my school is not listed?

Yes. The sign-up form allows you to type your school's name directly.

Can parents track the van live?

Live tracking is a planned feature. It requires GPS data, a backend and permission from the transport provider.

Are the login forms connected to a database?

No. They are front-end practice forms. Real accounts need secure authentication and a backend.
