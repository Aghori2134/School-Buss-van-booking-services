Daily Safety Checklist

Review these items before operating a school transport vehicle.

Visual checklist

Vehicle condition

Check tyres, brakes, lights, mirrors and other essential vehicle equipment.

Required documents

Confirm applicable licence, vehicle registration, insurance, permit and fitness documents.

Passenger safety

Follow safe boarding, seating, supervision and drop-off procedures.

Route readiness

Review the approved route, timings and any instructions from the operator.

If a vehicle has a safety defect or a required document is invalid, report it to the responsible operator and follow applicable rules before operating the vehicle.

Report a Vehicle or Route Issue

Contact your operator about a non-emergency issue.

This demonstration form does not send or store reports. For emergencies, contact the appropriate local emergency service or responsible transport authority.

Protect student information

Use student information only for authorised transport duties. Do not share route lists, personal details or account credentials with unauthorised people.
