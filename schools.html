Frequently asked questions

Schools Directory FAQs

Quick answers to common questions about schools and transport in Aurangabad.

Where can I find schools in Aurangabad?

This directory provides a sample of schools in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Maharashtra, including listings around Kanchanwadi, Shahnoorwadi, Waluj, Chilkalthana and Kat Kat Gate. Confirm each school's address using its official website or relevant school-affiliation records.

Does SchoolVan provide transport to every listed school?

Not necessarily. A school appearing in this directory does not confirm that a SchoolVan driver or route is available. Check the current route coverage and booking availability before making arrangements.

How can parents check a school's curriculum or affiliation?

Visit the school's official website. For CBSE schools, parents can also search the official CBSE SARAS affiliation directory. Confirm the school's current affiliation status rather than relying only on third-party listings.

Open official CBSE school directory
What should parents verify before booking a school van?

Confirm the pickup and drop-off arrangements, route, fare, vehicle documents, driver authorisation, emergency contact process and applicable school transport safety requirements.

Are school fees and admission vacancies shown here?

No. This directory focuses on school identification and location. Contact the school directly for current fees, admission criteria, available seats and academic information.

Planning your child's school commute?

Explore the booking page and confirm whether a suitable transport route is available for your school and pickup area.
